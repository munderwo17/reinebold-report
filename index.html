WEEK 1  |  The Recap Nobody Asked For By Jeff Reinebold

Well folks, pull up a folding chair. Week 1 is like the first day of training camp in the rain — everybody thinks they're a champion until the whistle blows and somebody's tight end lays an egg. We had fireworks, we had faceplants, and we had a man leave 51 points on his bench like loose change in a gas station parking lot. Grab a coffee. Reinebold's got the tape.

01The Games

Questionable or Kicked Liam Towers • 1-0
def.
Bang average
161.66 – 135.46

Game of the Week, and it wasn't close. "Questionable or Kicked" dropped a league-high 161.66 — nothing questionable about it. The backfield of Ashton Jeanty (32.7) and D'Andre Swift (32.4) ran like they owed somebody money, and Jaxon Smith-Njigba (26.2) was open all afternoon.

Bang average got a bell-cow game from Kenneth Walker III (34.1), but Jaylen Waddle (1.2) and George Kittle (3.2) showed up in invisibility cloaks.

You averaged 135 and still lost by 26. That's not "bang average," son — that's "bring a bigger gun." Waddle put up 1.2; I've seen more production out of the Gatorade table.

139.82 – 130.76

The heavyweight fight of the week. "AllInTheTush" got balance from Bijan Robinson (31.3), Zay Flowers (26.0) and a clean Jalen Hurts (24.72) — nobody under 5 in the lineup.

"Champ" nearly backed up the name behind Josh Allen's absurd 35.66 and Justin Jefferson (31.2) — 67 from two guys! — but J.K. Dobbins (3.6) and a 2.0 from Brandon Aubrey left just enough on the table.

Hey Champ — putting the trophy in your name doesn't win the trophy. Got 67 from two studs and still couldn't close. All hat, no cattle. Keep the emoji, fix the roster.
Sun God Eclipsed 1-0
def.
Cracked Out Uncle
156.1 – 103.96

"Sun God Eclipsed" eclipsed the field with 156.1, riding a breakout from Christian Watson (32.7), David Montgomery (28.9) out of the FLEX, and Trevor Lawrence (26.1).

Now the tragedy of the week: Ben Lawton's "Cracked Out Uncle" scored 103.96 while leaving Isaiah Likely (27.8) and Chuba Hubbard (23.7) STAPLED to the bench — 51.5 points on the couch. Started Chargers D/ST (0.0) and Cameron Dicker (2.0) instead.

Uncle, I've never — NEVER — seen a man punt 51 points into his own bench. Likely and Hubbard were begging to play. Lay off whatever you're cracked out on and set your lineup, pal.
Silence of the CeeDee Lambs Alex Farnell • 1-0
def.
Major Tuddy
120.82 – 106.6

Lowest-scoring affair of the week, but a W's a W. Carried by Derrick Henry's monstrous 35.3 and Trey McBride (24.5). Fittingly quiet elsewhere — Ja'Marr Chase (3.2) went silent.

Major Tuddy got tidy lines from CeeDee Lamb (15.4) and Breece Hall (19.8), but the Texans D/ST posted a MINUS 4.0. Yes, negative.

Major Tuddy, your defense scored negative four. That's not defense, that's a hostage situation. That mascot cost you the game single-handedly.
